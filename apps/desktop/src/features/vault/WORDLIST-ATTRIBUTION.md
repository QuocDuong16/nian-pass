Original source: https://www.eff.org/dice
Derivative source: https://github.com/keepassxreboot/keepassxc/tree/develop/share/wordlists

The bundled copy comes from the locally installed KeePassXC distribution's
`share/wordlists/eff_large.wordlist`, SHA-256:
`df895130803573b14caa684e8cba982a9ed21f4898c98c711f204acf600efedd`.
KeePassXC modified the original list, including replacing some offensive words.
